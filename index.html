🎯 Learning Outcomes

1

Determine the order of reaction with respect to each reactant by comparing initial-rate data from experimental trials.

2

Write the rate law for a reaction from experimental data and explain why orders are not taken from the balanced equation.

🔑 Key words
Rate law

Rate = k[A]m[B]n — links rate to concentrations.

Reaction order

The exponent (m, n) showing how much the rate depends on a reactant.

Method of initial rates

Compare starting rates of trials where only one concentration changes.
